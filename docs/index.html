    How it works

    1. Collect. Every morning a script asks the Federal Employment Agency's job search and the career pages of Hamburg employers for student-level jobs: Werkstudent, internship, thesis, junior and trainee roles.
    2. Read. Each new ad is read once. Rules pick out the job type, skills, hours and work mode; an AI model (Claude) writes a short English summary and judges how much German and English the ad really asks for, even when the ad itself is in German.
    3. Publish. The result is one file of jobs that this page filters right in your browser. Ads that disappear from their source are removed after a few days.

    "German needed" explained

    • Not needed The ad does not ask for German, or says it isn't required.
    • Basic / a plus Some German helps, or it is listed as nice to have.
    • Required Good or fluent German is required, or the ad is written in German only.

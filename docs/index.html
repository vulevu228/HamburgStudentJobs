    How it works

    1. Collect. Every morning a script asks the Federal Employment Agency, Arbeitnow and the career pages of Hamburg employers for student-level jobs: Werkstudent, internship, thesis, side jobs, junior and trainee roles. The same ad posted by several branches or sites is shown once.
    2. Read. Each new ad is read once. Rules pick out the job type, field, skills, hours, work mode and how much German the ad asks for. For English-friendly ads, an AI model (Claude) can add a short English summary and key requirements.
    3. Publish. The result is one file of jobs that this page filters right in your browser. Ads that disappear from their source are removed after a few days.

    "German needed" explained

    • Not needed The ad does not ask for German, or says it isn't required.
    • Basic / a plus Some German helps, or it is listed as nice to have.
    • Required Good or fluent German is required, or the ad is written in German only.

    Nothing you enter here leaves your browser. Saved jobs and skills are stored only on this device. The site counts visits anonymously (no cookies, no search terms) to see whether it is useful.
